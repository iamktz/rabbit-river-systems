Between knowing and doing

Spend your selling
time selling.

You know you should prospect and follow up. But the available time goes into finding names, researching, remembering the last conversation, or deciding what to say. The call gets pushed into tomorrow.

Rabbit River Sales is developing an action layer between the intention to sell and the actual human selling moment: practical preparation that helps you arrive ready to act.

Choose the right people.

Prospecting begins with sorting for fit. Use evidence to choose whom to approach and treat what you infer as a question to explore.

Have a reason to reach out.

Gather the useful context, prepare a short opening, and know what you want to learn. Preparation earns its place when it leads to contact.

Keep the next step moving.

Listen, qualify, ask clearly, record the result, and give the next action a date. Keep lines in the water as other conversations develop.

The work of selling

From making time
to making contact.

  1. Make Room

    Protect a realistic block of selling time.

  2. Get Ready

    Choose the contacts, gather context, and prepare the next action.

  3. Reach Out

    Make the call or send the considered message.

  4. Have the Conversation

    Listen, ask, and qualify whether there is a useful fit.

  5. Advance

    Ask for a specific next step and agree on what happens next.

  6. Follow Through

    Do what you promised, record the result, and follow up when due.

  7. Refill

    Find the next suitable prospects and keep the work moving.

These describe the seller’s work. Opportunity stages belong in your pipeline.

Prepare well. Act quickly. Ask clearly. Record the result. Move on.

Keep the human moment.

Conversation, listening, qualification, trust, asking, and commitment remain human responsibilities.

Work beside what you use.

Rabbit River is not building a CRM. The direction is to support your CRM, spreadsheet, or simple manual process with a useful next action.

Make activity count.

“CRM tourism” and “spreadsheet theater” consume time without creating meaningful customer contact. Keep the record useful and return to the work.
