What I examine.

  • Offer clarity: what is available, who it is for, what arrives, what it costs, and how to begin.
  • Customer friction: where a suitable visitor has to infer, hunt, hesitate, or take too large a step.
  • Evidence and trust: whether present-day claims have visible support.
  • Search and retrieval foundations: headings, descriptions, canonical URLs, internal links, crawler files, structured data, and share metadata.
  • Agent readiness: whether an assistant can reliably explain the business, fit, boundaries, and next action.
  • Basic accessibility signals: obvious reading, contrast, labeling, and interaction issues.
