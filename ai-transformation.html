The Rabbit River approach

Use the simplest thing that reliably works.

Start with the work.
Name the recurring delay, mistake, or manual step and the people it affects.
Use the simplest technology.
Try existing features, configuration, and supported connections before adding automation, AI, or custom software.
Make it earn its keep.
Compare the result with today’s process, including review, training, and maintenance.
