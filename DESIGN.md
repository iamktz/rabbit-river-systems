# Rabbit River Systems — Design Direction

## Working position

Rabbit River Systems finds overlooked opportunities—inside businesses and in the market—and turns them into practical systems, tools, and products.

The website leads with the clearest immediate commercial problem: businesses often have more opportunity than their current sales process captures. Rabbit River helps them see the leakage, decide what matters, and build the smallest useful solution.

Rabbit River is not presented as an “AI agency.” AI is part of the operating capability, alongside sales experience, systems thinking, technical depth, and product building.

## Primary audience and action

- Audience: owners and practical leaders of small and midsize businesses
- Visitor concern: “We need more leads / more sales / better visibility, but I do not know exactly where the problem is.”
- Primary action: start a conversation about missed opportunity
- Entry offer: Smart Sales Scan, subject to final naming and packaging

## Messaging hierarchy

### Hero headline

**Most sales aren’t lost. They’re mismanaged.**

This is intentionally more provocative than the rest of the voice. It should create recognition, not accuse the reader.

### Recommended supporting copy

**Before you spend more money finding leads, let’s stop the good ones from slipping away. Rabbit River builds practical sales systems, automations, and tools that help businesses respond faster, follow up better, and see what’s actually happening.**

### Short alternative

**The opportunities are already there. Rabbit River helps you respond faster, follow up better, and turn more interest into revenue.**

### Brand-level statement

**We find overlooked opportunities and turn them into practical systems, tools, and products.**

### M3 operating framework

- **Mine it:** uncover the useful problem, unmet need, or hidden opportunity
- **Make it:** build the smallest effective system, tool, or product
- **Move it:** put it into use, take it to market, measure it, and improve it

M3 unifies client services and Rabbit River’s own products. It should appear below the commercial homepage story, not replace it.

## Brand character

- Practical, not theoretical
- Experienced, not corporate
- Inventive, not trendy
- Direct, not aggressive
- Technical, but human
- Curious, without appearing unfocused
- Commercially grounded, without sounding like a sales agency

## Visual concept

### Working name

**The Operator’s Field Journal**

The page should feel like a clear commercial diagnosis documented by someone who has worked in sales, technology, engineering, and AI—not like a generic consultancy template or an AI-generated SaaS landing page.

The visual language combines:

- editorial authority;
- industrial and technical precision;
- warm, tactile materials;
- sparse diagnostic notation;
- a single decisive action color.

## Reference board

### 1. Aboard — atmosphere and material

Reference: https://styles.refero.design/style/7b083729-e694-4b66-82a3-befb08451722

Borrow:

- warm paper against very dark documentary sections;
- editorial “field journal under industrial light” quality;
- thin interfaces, hairline borders, and minimal elevation;
- candid, work-oriented imagery if photography is introduced;
- orange reserved for action and active signals.

Avoid:

- translucent floating navigation;
- excessive atmosphere that competes with the offer;
- warehouse imagery unless it supports a real story.

### 2. Orderful — commercial discipline

Reference: https://styles.refero.design/style/d95a35c2-d3f7-49e7-9ba7-282d52d3211d

Borrow:

- restrained B2B composition;
- light-to-medium headline weights that signal confidence without shouting;
- small uppercase operational labels;
- consistent geometry and an explicit spacing system;
- scarce color used to make the next action unmistakable.

Avoid:

- making Rabbit River feel like enterprise logistics software;
- information density that overwhelms a small-business visitor.

### 3. Antimetal — systems intelligence

Reference: https://styles.refero.design/style/9f9a4a4f-1a27-47ca-a65b-68b9850a84e4

Borrow:

- technical-journal structure;
- numbered sections, annotations, pull quotes, and sparse diagrams;
- warm cream canvas with regular-weight serif authority;
- flat components separated by rules and whitespace.

Avoid:

- quietness so extreme that the commercial offer disappears;
- decorative diagrams with no relationship to the content.

### 4. Parallel Web Systems — typography and system graphics

Reference: https://styles.refero.design/style/32845f27-6b24-48be-af25-8e664f826b30

Borrow:

- serif for reading, mono for labels and operational details;
- cream rather than pure white;
- near-square structural geometry;
- data, paths, and system states used as the graphic language;
- hairlines and whitespace instead of floating cards.

Avoid:

- terminal aesthetics;
- large blocks of fake data;
- a visual identity that reads as developer infrastructure.

### 5. Waabi — documentary-industrial proof

Reference: https://styles.refero.design/style/91174f53-6770-4398-b3e7-ad14b1c39b6d

Borrow:

- real, specific subjects rather than aspirational stock imagery;
- asymmetric layouts with deliberate empty space;
- warm concrete surfaces and carbon information panels;
- photography as evidence rather than decoration.

Avoid:

- oversized soft rounding;
- a scale that implies a venture-funded industrial company.

### 6. Harvest — accessibility and warmth

Reference: https://styles.refero.design/style/1eee9aa2-1e23-4675-9f6e-fb98c93969bd

Borrow:

- approachable warmth;
- easy reading and generous breathing room;
- strong editorial serif/sans relationship;
- orange as a clear, optimistic action color.

Avoid:

- soft floating-card UI;
- friendliness that reduces technical or commercial authority;
- rounded SaaS-template styling.

## Proposed design system

### Color

The existing palette is directionally right but should become more disciplined and less generically “Pacific Northwest green.”

- **Field Paper:** `#F3F0E8` — primary canvas
- **Clean Paper:** `#FBF9F4` — inset reading surfaces
- **Carbon:** `#171B19` — primary text and dark sections
- **River Ink:** `#173D35` — brand depth, used selectively
- **Slate:** `#66706B` — secondary copy
- **Rule:** `#D8D6CE` — borders and diagrams
- **Signal Orange:** `#F06A2A` — actions, active states, critical annotations only

No gradients. Avoid multiple decorative greens. Orange always means action, emphasis, or live opportunity.

### Typography

- Display: a characterful editorial serif at regular weight
- Body: a highly readable humanist or neutral sans
- Operational labels: a restrained mono face

The serif supplies experience and judgment. The sans explains. The mono labels systems, evidence, and process. Mono should never dominate body copy.

### Geometry

- Structural radius: `0–4px`
- Buttons: `2–4px`, not pills
- Hairline borders: `1px`
- Little or no box shadow
- Surfaces separated by tone, rules, and whitespace
- Section spacing should follow a consistent 8px-derived scale

### Graphic language

Use diagrams that illustrate real commercial movement:

- inquiry → response → conversation → decision;
- visible opportunity versus lost opportunity;
- gaps, delays, handoffs, and follow-up loops;
- Mine → Make → Move;
- field-note annotations and numbered observations.

The graphics should make the business problem easier to understand. No abstract AI orbs, neural-network clichés, glowing grids, fake dashboards, or decorative river waves.

### Photography

Photography is optional at first. When used, it should be documentary proof:

- real work environments;
- tools, systems, machinery, notebooks, screens, and hands at work;
- the founder in a credible working context;
- restrained, warm, slightly desaturated treatment.

Avoid staged handshakes, smiling office teams, generic mountain scenery, robots, and futuristic AI imagery.

### Motion

- subtle state changes and directional reveals only;
- diagrams may trace or resolve once as they enter view;
- no parallax spectacle;
- honor reduced-motion preferences;
- the page should feel stable before it feels animated.

## Homepage structure

1. Commercial hero: lost opportunities and a direct next step
2. Recognition: ordinary places where opportunities disappear
3. Diagnostic offer: the Smart Sales Scan
4. What Rabbit River can build or improve
5. M3: the operating framework connecting services and products
6. Products/workshop: evidence that Rabbit River builds, not merely advises
7. Founder credibility: sales, systems, technology, and pre-hype AI experience
8. Direct conversational CTA

## Logo direction

Do not finalize the logo before the page’s visual system is tested.

Explore a compact maker’s mark rather than a literal rabbit illustration:

- watershed or confluence notation;
- route, current, or opportunity-path symbol;
- surveyor’s mark;
- two paths resolving into one useful direction;
- an `RR` construction that can also read as flow or movement.

The mark must work in one color, at favicon scale, on paper, and beside the full name. Avoid mascots, detailed landscapes, circular startup badges, and generic circuit-brain imagery.

## Explicit exclusions

- neon AI gradients
- glassmorphism
- oversized pill-shaped everything
- generic “unlock growth” copy
- invented client logos or unverified metrics
- fake application screenshots
- stock-photo corporate optimism
- rustic outdoor branding
- excessive rabbit or river imagery
- positioning Kevin as a social-media-style AI guru

## Founder narrative note

The homepage should establish breadth without telling the entire story. A later About page can describe Kevin Butler’s hands-on generative AI use since ChatGPT’s public release in November 2022, earlier image-generation experimentation, collaboration with Nvidia, and advocacy to Lenovo senior management around workstation AI solutions circa 2018–2019.

The Nvidia “I AM AI” shirts and scornful laughter after asking when AI would take over the world is a memorable supporting anecdote. It belongs in a longer founder story, article, talk, or video—not in the homepage hero.

## First redesign pass

The first implementation should validate the direction with:

- the new commercial hero and supporting copy;
- sharper typography and tokenized color;
- a real opportunity-flow diagram in place of decorative river lines;
- a more disciplined diagnostic panel;
- the revised section hierarchy;
- a provisional text-forward identity while logo exploration remains open.

Do not broaden into a full product catalog or a detailed About page until this direction works on the homepage.
