You don't have a lead problem. You have a leak.

More advertising just pours more water into the same bucket. And fixing it doesn't mean a CRM migration, a new system to learn, or some giant AI project. You keep working the way you already work. You plug the one place it's actually going out.

The hard part isn't the fix. It's that most owners guess wrong about which one is costing them — and the loudest problem is almost never the expensive one.
