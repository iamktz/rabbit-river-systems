Get Found Where Customers Are Looking.

Make your online presence clearer, more consistent, and easier for the right people to discover.

The problem

Good work can be hard to find.

Your customers may hear about you through a referral, a local search, a directory, or an AI answer. Each route should lead to a clear and consistent account of the business.

The message is unclear
People cannot quickly tell what you do, who you help, where you work, or why the service fits their situation.
The public picture is incomplete
Business listings, contact details, service areas, and descriptions disagree or omit important information.
The path ends too soon
Thin service pages, weak page structure, unanswered questions, and unclear calls to action make it harder to evaluate the business.
A bounded starting point

Check the places customers actually use.

Choose one service and one customer area. Review the main service page, relevant listings, and the path to an inquiry. Look for facts you can correct and useful information you can add before buying more promotion.

Visibility review · Available by request
Agree a small set of public pages and listings to examine. The output is observed gaps and a prioritized list of corrections, not a continuing agency retainer.
Local discovery and information checks
Check names, contact information, service areas, links, and basic search hygiene. Resolve conflicting signals and gaps in useful content.
Smart Sales Scan · In development
An outside-in scan may help surface visible friction. It cannot establish how much business was lost without evidence from you.
Start with what is true

Publish the answer your customer needs.

Illustrative example

“Do you work in our area?”

A service page that names the actual coverage area and explains how to request a quote is more useful than a general promise to serve everyone. Make the same facts consistent wherever the business is listed.
