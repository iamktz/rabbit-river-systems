Get Found Where Customers Are Looking.

Make it easier for the right people to discover that you exist, wherever they look for a business like yours.

The problem

Good work is not always easy to discover.

A referral may send someone to a search engine, a map, your reviews, or a social profile. Incomplete information or an unclear service page can make a suitable business hard to recognize.

Look where customers look
Start with how your customers actually find businesses like yours. Review local search, Google Business Profile where relevant, directories, social discovery, referrals, and your website.
Make the public picture consistent
Check names, contact details, opening hours, services, coverage areas, and links. Complete information and credible reviews help people recognize and evaluate the business.
Make useful pages discoverable
Check basic search visibility, page titles, internal links, and clear service descriptions. Include the questions, locations, and practical details customers need.
Findability Reality Check · Available by request

Find the gaps before adding another channel.

Start with one service and the people or area it serves. Agree a bounded set of pages, listings, and discovery routes to examine.

  1. Bring the starting picture

    Your website, relevant public profiles, service area, and what customers say helped them find you.

  2. Trace the likely routes

    Look for missing information, inconsistent signals, weak reputation evidence, and dead ends.

  3. Prioritize the corrections

    Receive a short list of observed gaps, their likely effect, and which to address first.

  4. Check what changed

    Agree a useful visibility or customer-understanding signal. Discovery improvements do not guarantee inquiries.

A practical improvement

Help people recognize a fit.

Illustrative example

“Do you repair heat pumps in our town?”

A page naming the equipment you actually service and the places you cover is more useful than “all your comfort needs.” Make those facts consistent with your listings.

Keep your website and business information current as well as your platform profiles. Do not build your entire presence on attention you cannot control.
