Make Sense of the Noise.

Sort search, social, AI, and vendor claims into what matters now and what can wait.

The problem

Every platform has a new urgent recommendation.

Search changes. Social platforms shift. Vendors promise that AI will handle the content, email, website, and everything else. Keeping up can become a job before any customer benefits.

What materially affects your business?
Start with how your customers find, evaluate, and contact you. A change matters when it affects that path or the work behind it.
What needs action now?
Broken contact paths, incorrect public information, or a channel your customers rely on deserve attention before an interesting new tool.
What can wait?
An unproven platform, a weakly supported prediction, or a capability without a useful job can go on a watch list.
Marketing Priorities Brief · Available by request

A short list beats another stream of advice.

Bring a few claims, tools, or changes you are trying to assess. Agree which decisions the brief should help you make. The goal is a plain-language view of the impact, uncertainty, and next action.

Act
Identify a concrete problem and the smallest sensible response.
Test
Name the uncertainty, a limited experiment, and what would make it worth continuing.
Wait
Record why the change can wait and what evidence would bring it back into consideration.
Keep useful knowledge close

Learn enough to make the next decision.

SEO, answer-engine optimization, content, and AI workflows deserve clear explanations. They do not all need a new subscription or a campaign this week.

A useful filter

What would change for our customer?

Ask what the recommendation would make easier, which current problem it solves, what it would cost to adopt, and what evidence supports the claim.
