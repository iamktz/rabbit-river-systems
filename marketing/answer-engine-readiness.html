Get Ready for Answer Engines.

Make your business knowledge easier to find and understand while keeping strong SEO fundamentals.

Discovery is changing

SEO is not dead. The questions are getting longer.

AI-powered search tools can assemble answers, compare options, and point people toward businesses. Customers may evaluate you through those answers as well as through a traditional search result.

Keep the fundamentals: useful visible content, accessible pages, clear internal links, accurate business information, and sound search hygiene. Google says its existing SEO practices still apply to its AI search features; there is no special AI markup requirement.

Source: Google Search Central: AI features and your website. Being eligible does not guarantee indexing, inclusion, citation, or recommendation.

The public knowledge layer

More than a shop window. A warehouse of useful answers.

A shop window gives a quick impression. A well-organized warehouse holds what people need and makes it findable. Your website can do both: introduce the business and preserve the service knowledge customers need to choose well.

Think of it as the public data layer of the business: accurate explanations of services, fit, process, limitations, pricing factors, and policies. Publish useful knowledge with clear headings, links, and evidence. Structured data, where appropriate, should reflect what visitors can actually read.

A practical preparation sequence

Find the gap between what you know and what is public.

  1. Gather what the business knows

    Collect recurring customer questions and the team’s reliable answers.

  2. Inspect what is public

    Read the website, relevant listings, and other discoverable sources.

  3. Compare the two

    Ask which customer questions the public information can actually answer.

  4. Identify the missing knowledge

    Separate useful gaps from information that should stay private.

  5. Publish, clarify, or structure

    Turn approved knowledge into specific answers, service explanations, and linked resources.

  6. Review and improve

    Keep facts current and check whether people and research tools can understand them.

Customer Question & Knowledge-Gap Diagnostic · In development

Your team may already know what the website is missing.

This working diagnostic may examine FAQs, recurring sales conversations, customer questions, service facts, policies, technical knowledge, process information, and common objections. It is intended to identify useful answers that are absent or unclear in public.

Use only information you are authorized to review. Remove private customer details and have the business approve the public explanation. The diagnostic is not released self-service software.
