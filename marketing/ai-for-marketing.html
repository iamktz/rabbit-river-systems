← Back to MarketingHow Should I Use AI for Marketing?

Do Better Work, Not Just More Work.

Use AI to understand, prepare, organize, and accelerate the work that matters.

The problem

AI has made marketing easier to produce. It has not made a business more relevant.

More drafts do not tell you who the customer is, why they should care, or whether the claim is true. AI lowers the cost of saying something. That increases the value of having something worth saying.

Understand
Research a topic, examine approved meeting notes or transcripts, group repeated customer questions, and compare campaign evidence. Check sources and conclusions.
Prepare
Summarize, outline, draft, reuse useful material, and prepare review responses. A person checks accuracy, tone, permissions, and purpose before publication.
Organize
Maintain resource libraries, sort internal knowledge, prepare reports, and surface gaps. Keep a responsible owner for the finished work.
AI Marketing Workflow Review · Available by request

Choose one recurring job worth improving.

Use AI to understand, prepare, organize, and accelerate. Do not use it to fill the internet with things nobody needed.

  1. Define useful work

    Pick a repeated task and the result it serves. Describe the current effort and quality standard.

  2. Select appropriate inputs

    Use approved material and only the access required. Remove private details that the task does not need.

  3. Try a bounded workflow

    Agree what AI prepares, what a person reviews, and who decides whether anything is published.

  4. Count the whole effort

    Include checking and corrections. Keep the approach only if it improves usefulness, quality, or total time.

Take away a proposed workflow for one task: approved inputs, what AI prepares, who reviews it, and how to compare time and quality with today’s process. Prompt kits and customer-question analysis software remain in development.

Useful work before more output

Turn repeated questions into a better answer.

Illustrative example

Several customers ask what happens during an installation.

AI can group approved questions and outline an explanation from your process notes. A knowledgeable person checks the facts, adds useful detail, and decides what belongs on the website. The result should answer a real question, not fill a publishing calendar.
