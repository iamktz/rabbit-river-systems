# Current website direction — September 17, 2026

This direction supersedes older positioning and speculative product names in DESIGN.md and previous redesign briefs.

Guiding principles, in order: **Wise. Nimble. Fruitful.**

Rabbit River Systems remains broader than sales. Sales, Marketing, and AI are visible pillars. Tools & Resources contains usable material; About and the twenty-minute conversation remain accessible.

Rabbit River Sales is developing an action layer between the intention to sell and the actual human selling moment. The audience is owners, founders, and small teams with limited selling time. AI supports preparation and administration; humans retain judgment, conversation, qualification, trust, asking, and commitment.

Human work: Make Room → Get Ready → Reach Out → Have the Conversation → Advance → Follow Through → Refill.

Opportunity state is separate: Prospect → Contacted → Engaged → Qualified → Active → Committed; outcomes include Won, Lost, Passed, and Dormant / Parked.

Operating maxim: Prepare well. Act quickly. Ask clearly. Record the result. Move on.

The seven sales field guides are available. Sales tools and diagnostics remain in development; there is no sales software catalog or CRM product. Any consulting engagement requires a bounded scope and agreed terms.

The Website Clarity and AI Readiness Review remains the existing paid service. Its intake/payment implementation remains deferred.

Production is static HTML/CSS in this repository, deployed through GitHub to Cloudflare Pages. Do not publish through Sites or revive the unused framework remnants.
