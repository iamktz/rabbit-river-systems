Not a sales problem

Most owners have never looked at what their AI tools are allowed to do.

They don't just answer questions anymore. They read your files, change records, send messages, and act through other systems on your behalf. Nobody decided that in one sitting — it accumulated, one convenient connection at a time.

You don't have a lead problem. You have a leak.

More advertising just pours more water into the same bucket. And fixing it doesn't mean a CRM migration, a new system to learn, or some giant AI project — you keep working the way you already work, and plug the one place it's going out.

The hard part is that most owners guess wrong about which one. The loudest problem is almost never the expensive one.
