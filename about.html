About Kevin

A point of view.
A reason behind it.

You don’t need another list of things your business could do. You need a clear recommendation about what is worth doing next—and someone willing to explain why.

That’s what I bring to Rabbit River Systems. I’m Kevin Butler. I work with owners on sales, marketing, and AI: finding where the work gets stuck, questioning the obvious answer, and turning a useful idea into something you can put to work.

I have opinions. You should expect them. You should also expect me to show my reasoning, name the tradeoffs, and change my mind when the evidence calls for it.

The recommendation has to survive contact with your business.

More than twenty-five years in technology sales, including HP and Lenovo, taught me to look beyond whether a solution sounds good. Can the team use it? Will it fit the way the business works? What happens when it doesn’t?

I worked with companies whose own products depended on HP technology, alongside their executives, engineers, and operations teams. Those decisions had consequences for what they could sell and support. My earlier work in electronics, technical teaching, and field service helped me ask the questions a sales presentation can leave out.

That is the experience I bring to your decisions: connecting what the technology can do with what your customers and your business actually need.

You should be able to question the advice.

“AI can do that” is the beginning of a discussion, not a business case. More leads won’t fix a follow-up problem. A clearer website may help more than another campaign. The right answer depends on what is actually getting in the way.

I’ll tell you what I see, what I’m assuming, and what I’d check before spending more. If a purchase isn’t necessary, I’ll say so. If something is outside my experience, I’ll say that too.

Look at the work before you take my word for it.

Credibility needs more than a confident About page. Read a field guide, try the website self-check, or look at the sample review. You can judge whether the thinking is clear and the recommendations are useful before we discuss working together.
