The approach

A clear scope.
A useful finish.

Small, focused work begins with understanding what would actually help.

First, we talk.

If I’ve sent you an email or a video, we can start with those observations. If you found me another way, tell me what brought you here. I’ll ask questions and share what I’d look at first.

You don’t need to prepare a presentation, send records, or give me account access for the first conversation.

Then, we choose a next step.

It might be a resource you can use yourself. It might be a defined diagnostic or another small deliverable. It might be work for a different specialist. If I don’t see a useful fit, I’ll say so.

Before any paid work, we agree on what you’ll receive, the price, and the delivery date. The conversation itself doesn’t commit you to a purchase.

The work has an endpoint.

Rabbit River is built around useful deliverables. A diagnostic should give you specific findings and a clear handoff. A tool or worksheet should have an understandable job.

I don’t offer ongoing website maintenance, manage your IT organization, or coordinate an open-ended implementation. Where findings need a web developer or another specialist, the handoff should make that next conversation easier.

Modern tools. Human judgment.

I use AI to research, analyze, and build. It can produce mistakes as readily as useful results. For a diagnostic, I review the generated findings before delivery and distinguish what was observed from what still needs checking.

A website scan can identify accessible information and visible problems. It cannot reveal everything happening inside a business or guarantee that a search platform will recommend it.
