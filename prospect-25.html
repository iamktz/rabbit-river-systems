How Prospect 25 works

Know. Do. Decide.

Know

Capture territory, services, desired account characteristics, account economics, exclusions, capacity, and useful fit signals.

Do

Research, filter, compare, and organize likely-fit accounts using public information, software, and AI-assisted research.

Decide

Return an explainable ranked set of prospects so the owner can pursue, nurture, or pass with a reason.

What you receive

Put scarce selling time where it belongs.

Prospect 25 is designed to help you decide where scarce selling time should go. Each account comes with reasoning, evidence, and a useful next step.

  • 25 researched commercial prospects
  • Clear explanation of how each prospect fits your profile, based on public evidence
  • Useful public evidence and fit signals
  • Likely decision-maker or contact path
  • Suggested first-touch angle
  • Important unknowns to verify
  • Pursue / Nurture / Pass guidance
  • A short “Start Here” group showing which prospects deserve attention first
