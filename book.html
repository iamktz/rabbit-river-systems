Twenty minutes.

Tell me what's going on and I'll tell you what I'd look at first. Phone or video, whichever you prefer.

What we'll talk about

Whatever's actually bothering you. Most people come in with something on the sales side — work coming in faster than it gets handled, quotes going quiet, a pipeline they can't see. That's the common one, and it's what most of this site is about.

Plenty of it isn't sales, though. A process that eats half a day every week. A question about whether some AI tool is worth the noise or not. Something you've been chewing on for a month and can't quite name yet. All fair game.

You describe what's happening. I ask a handful of questions. Then I tell you where I'd start — and if it's something you can go handle yourself in an afternoon, I'll say that, because it's usually true and you'd find out anyway.

Worth having handy

  • A rough sense of how work comes in and who touches it first.
  • One recent thing that went sideways — a job, a quote, a process, anything.
  • What you've already tried, if you've tried something.

Nothing to prepare in advance, nothing to send me, and I don't need access to anything of yours.

Then what

Usually one of three things. You go do the thing yourself. Or we find there's something worth a closer look and I tell you what that would involve. Or it turns out I'm not the right person, in which case I'll say so and point you at what I'd do instead.
