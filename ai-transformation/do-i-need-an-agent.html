Know When an Agent Helps.

Choose the right level of automation, assistance, or bounded action for the job.

Start with the work

Do not automate confusion.

An agent is not the next mandatory software purchase. Decide what the job requires, how success is observed, and what authority you are prepared to delegate. A simple rule or a human-guided assistant may be enough.

Automation
Fixed rules and predictable steps. For example, copy an approved form submission into a work queue.
Assistant
Helps a person think, draft, analyze, or prepare. The person directs the work and decides what to use.
Bounded agent
Can choose and carry out limited multi-step actions toward a defined objective, within specified tools, permissions, and stopping conditions.
Agentic system
Coordinates several actions, tools, systems, or agents. That can add capability, but also more things to test, observe, and maintain.

These are practical distinctions, not a universal naming standard. For the underlying workflow/agent distinction, see Anthropic’s explanation of workflows and agents.

Where an agent may fit

Repeated work. Clear boundaries. Observable outcomes.

A stronger candidate

A repeated multi-step workflow with usable data, a clear objective, bounded access, an observable result, and a person who can review exceptions.

A reason to simplify first

An unclear process, unreliable data, a rare task, a high-risk decision, or a workflow that changes every time. More autonomy can amplify those problems.

Illustrative example

Research a shortlist. Do not contact anyone yet.

A bounded agent might gather public facts against stated criteria and prepare a source-backed shortlist. A person checks the evidence and chooses the next action. Sending messages would be a separate permission, not an assumed part of research.

Authority is part of the design

What can it read, change, send, or spend?

Set the boundaries before connecting tools: permitted information, allowed actions, human approval points, time and spending limits, and when to stop. Keep an action record and a way to handle errors. Read access can expose information even when nothing is changed.

Test normal cases and failures. Check whether the system can recognize when it lacks information or permission and hand the decision back to a person.
