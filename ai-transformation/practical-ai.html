Use AI Where It Improves the Work.

Find recurring tasks where AI could save time, improve quality, or create value you can observe.

The problem

AI is useful task by task.

AI is not a capability you simply “have.” Its usefulness changes task by task. A strong draft does not establish that the same tool can reliably check a spreadsheet or make a customer commitment.

Understand and prepare
Research, information retrieval, summarization, document analysis, and meeting preparation can become easier when sources are available and checked.
Draft and organize
Drafting, rewriting, classification, reporting, and customer-support preparation can reduce effort while a person reviews the result.
Analyze and build
Spreadsheet analysis, coding, small utilities, and workflow support can be useful. Validate important calculations, test changes, and include review time in the comparison.

Use AI first where mistakes are visible, reversible, and cheap. Increase controls as consequences increase.

AI Opportunity Review · Available by request

Find one to three recurring tasks worth testing.

We agree a small set of workflows to examine. Bring examples, current effort, and what a good result looks like. The review compares possible interventions rather than assuming every task needs AI.

Leave alone / Improve the process
Keep a working task as it is, or remove an unnecessary step and clarify who does what.
Existing feature / Simple automation
Use a capability you already have, or rules for stable, predictable steps.
AI assistant / Integration
Use AI to help a person interpret or prepare material, or connect systems when the handoff is the actual problem.
Mini app / Bounded agent
Consider a small tool for proven friction. Consider an agent only when variable, multi-step work justifies AI choosing the next step.

Take away a short opportunity list, the simplest suitable approach for each task, and a test plan: inputs, baseline, review owner, success measure, and stopping condition. Finding no justified AI use case is a valid result.

Prove the improvement

Count checking and correction as part of the work.

Illustrative example

Prepare a service summary from approved notes.

AI drafts the summary and flags missing facts. A knowledgeable person checks it before it reaches the customer. Compare total preparation and review time, omissions, and usefulness with the current process.

Human judgment owns consequential decisions. Drafting a response is different from sending it, changing a record, or promising a refund. Any implementation needs its own scope, permissions, and testing.

A useful starting point

If the job seems to require multiple steps and tool access, first check whether an assistant or ordinary automation would be enough.
