← Back to Tech & AIWhat Technology Actually Matters for My Business?

Know What Deserves Your Attention.

Separate what affects your business from what is merely new. Decide what to act on, test, or leave alone.

The problem

New is not the same as useful to your business.

AI releases, software changes, vendor claims, and competitor choices compete for your attention. Start with the work you need to do, not the pressure to keep up.

Important
A change affects a real dependency, service, customer requirement, or operating risk. Understand the consequence and timing.
Useful
It could improve recurring work. Define a small test before making a commitment.
Interesting
There may be potential, but no current job or evidence justifies the effort. Record what would make it worth revisiting.
Irrelevant for now
It does not solve a problem you have or fit the way your business works. It can be ignored.
A technology reality check

Turn the claim into a business question.

We can talk through one proposed change, what you already use, and the evidence needed for a decision. If a deeper review would help, agree its scope and price first.

  1. Name the job

    What would get faster, clearer, more reliable, or less costly? Who would notice?

  2. Inspect the claim

    Distinguish a demonstrated result from a product announcement or a result from a different kind of business.

  3. Check the simpler option

    Could an existing feature, a setting, a template, or a process change do enough?

  4. Choose the next decision

    Act on a real dependency, test a plausible improvement, or wait with a reason. Set a point to review new evidence.

No purchase required

A useful answer can be “keep what you have.”

Illustrative example

A vendor says every team needs an AI assistant.

If your team regularly searches scattered service notes, that is a task to examine. If the information is already easy to find, an additional assistant may solve little. A competitor buying one does not establish your need.

A useful starting point

For a specific task, the AI Opportunity Review compares practical options. For a purchase or renewal, examine its full value and burden.
