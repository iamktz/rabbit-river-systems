What does your
website leave unclear?

Your services, your location, how to reach you. The basics should be easy for people and search systems to find and understand.

A focused look at what’s visible.

I’m developing an AI-assisted diagnostic for business websites. The aim is to check publicly accessible information, identify specific issues, and turn the findings into a useful handoff.

The prototype is being refined. The final checklist, price, and delivery time are not yet set.

The intended deliverable

Evidence. Priorities. Next steps.

Each finding should show what was observed, where it appeared, why it deserves attention, and what your web person should check or change. Uncertain findings should be identified as uncertain.

What I’m testing.

  • Whether services, service areas, and contact details are clear and consistent.
  • Whether important pages and information are accessible to crawlers.
  • Whether visible links and contact paths create avoidable friction.
  • Whether the report separates consequential issues from minor housekeeping.

Search is changing. Clear information still matters.

Fixing inaccessible or contradictory information is useful work. It does not guarantee rankings, inclusion in an AI answer, or additional leads. I won’t present a simulated agent’s opinion as proof of how Google or another search service will treat your business.

You get a handoff, not another dependency.

The diagnostic is intended for you and whoever maintains your website. It does not include implementation, hosting, maintenance, or an ongoing retainer.

If you’d like to discuss whether this approach fits your situation, start with a conversation. We’ll talk about what you need before deciding on any paid work.
